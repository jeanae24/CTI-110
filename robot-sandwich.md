# Steps to Making PBJ Sandwich

## Assumptions
- 2 slices of bread
- 2 tablespoons peanut butter
- 2 tablespoons jam or jelly
- Butter knife
- Plate or clean surface

## Instructions
1. Set up the bread. Lay 2 slices of bread side by side on a plate or clean surface.
2. Apply peanut butter. Apply 2 tablespoons of peanut butter on the butterknife. Spread the peanut butter from the butter knife onto the first slice of bread and spread it out evenly. 
3. Apply jam or jelly. Apply 2 tablespoons of jelly on the butter knife onto the second slice of bread and spread it out evenly.
4. Combine the slices. Put both of the pieces of bread together. Cut the sandwich with the butter knife down the middle. 

## Done when
- the sandwich is cut in half
